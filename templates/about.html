About E-Shop

Empowering Sellers and Buyers through a modern, secure, and interactive e-commerce platform.

Our Mission

To create a seamless, role-based marketplace that gives sellers full control over product management while offering buyers verified products, transparent ratings, and real-time interaction.

Interactive Tech

Built with Flask, SQLite, and Bootstrap 5, featuring embedded Three.js 3D web visuals and an option-driven chatbot for instant system analytics access.

User Roles & Permissions

Admin
Predefined system access, full platform analytics dashboard access, and complete store management rights.
Seller
Register and manage inventory using full Product CRUD operations (Add, Edit, Delete, Read).
Buyer
Browse product catalog, view detailed seller contacts, place orders, and submit verified ratings & reviews.
